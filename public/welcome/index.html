04 — THE PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Clear answers.
Real boundaries.

Ownership should be understandable before you sign up.

What does “sovereign” mean here?

Control over your agent’s hosting, a choice of supported model providers, and the ability to export your data. It does not mean that nobody else can ever access data, or that every external service runs in Finland.

Where will my data be stored and processed?

The planned agent hosting location is Finland on UpCloud. Requests sent to a model are processed under that provider’s terms and in its processing locations. Connected services can also receive data when used. The final provider list will be published before payments open.

Who can access my data?

Access depends on the hosting, service administration, and inference setup. We have not yet published the verified access and retention policies for this offer. Those details must be available before signup; this page does not claim end-to-end encryption or operator-blind access.

Can I move my agent somewhere else?

The backend includes encrypted export and restore tooling. A complete migration of a working agent still needs production acceptance testing. Do not treat it as a verified one-click transfer yet. The downloadable sample above illustrates useful export contents; it is not a verified export from a live account.

Can I run the models, data, and inference locally?

That is the goal: an agent you can run on a computer you control, including its models and inference. This launch offers managed hosting in Finland with external model connections. A complete local setup and verified migration are not current claims.

Can I build my own alternative?

Yes—that is the freedom we want to support. We want open-source software, documented formats, and a practical path to running things yourself. The platform code is public under Apache-2.0, with deployment and restore documentation. Production deployment and complete migration still need their acceptance checks. OpenClaw, model licenses, and provider terms remain separate.

What is included in €25/month?

The founding offer covers managed agent hosting and service maintenance. Inference is extra: use your own API key and pay the provider directly, or buy prepaid credit. The first 50 customers keep the €25 monthly base price for 12 months. Taxes and the recurring total will be shown before payment.

How do cancellation and deletion work?

The intended offer is monthly billing with cancellation of future renewals. The exact access period after cancellation, export window, deletion process, and backup retention still need to be confirmed and published before signup. Cancelling billing and deleting stored data are separate actions.

Is the walkthrough a real product recording?

No. It is an illustrative example of the intended experience. A recording from a working account will replace it after the task, memory, and export flow have been verified.
