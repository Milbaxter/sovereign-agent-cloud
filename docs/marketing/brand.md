# Brand guide

The visual identity and voice used by `/welcome/`, the journal, the account portal and the share image. Claims still follow the [privacy and security claims policy](../PRIVACY-SECURITY.md); beautiful copy never outranks accurate copy.

## Positioning

**Freedom is the feature.** Grok Bot, Muse and the other big assistants are built by platforms, around platforms. Your Agent is built around the person using it: open-source software, a model of their choosing, and a context designed to travel.

Say it through four freedoms, each with an honest status:

| Freedom            | What it means                                             | Status to show                      |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| To choose          | Bring your own key or buy prepaid inference; change later | Built                               |
| To inspect         | Platform source public under Apache-2.0                   | Public today                        |
| To leave           | Encrypted export and restore tooling                      | Export built · migration in testing |
| To run it yourself | Local models, local inference, your hardware              | Where we’re heading                 |

Compare on questions, not on alleged competitor gaps. “Five questions to ask any AI assistant” invites the reader to test everyone, including us. Do not state what Grok Bot or Muse lack.

## Voice

- Warm, plain and a little lyrical. Short sentences. Nordic calm rather than startup urgency.
- Metaphors of home, horizon and room to breathe: “rooms you rent in someone else’s house”, “a home of your own”, “never the only door”.
- Name limits in the same breath as promises: operator access, separate inference, illustrative demo. Candour is part of the brand.
- Approved lines: “Your personal AI, set free.” · “Freedom is the feature.” · “Your freedom is the point.” · “Use it. Change it. Make it yours.” · “Your personal AI should belong to you.”

## Logo

`public/welcome/logo-mark.svg` (also `favicon.svg`). The brackets are your space; the horizon runs straight through them, because your space should never be a cage; the sun rising on it is what comes next. Keep the mark on its own gradient tile, at least 16 px, with clear space of a quarter of its width. The wordmark is lowercase “your agent” in Fraunces with a sun-coloured period.

## Type

Fraunces (display, with italics for the emotional half of a headline) and Inter (body), bundled locally under the SIL Open Font License in `public/welcome/fonts/`. No remote font requests.

## Imagery

All photography is the AI-generated illustrative Nordic coast (`nordic-coast.*`) and crops of it (`nordic-horizon.webp`, `nordic-pine.webp`). Show it in arches and full-bleed bands. The FAQ discloses that the images are illustrative; never caption them as a data centre or specific location. `og-image.jpg` is the 1200×630 share card, regenerated from the same assets.
