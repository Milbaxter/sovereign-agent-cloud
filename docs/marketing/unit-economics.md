# Can €25/month support the service?

The price is a launch hypothesis. No server size, actual vendor invoice, tax treatment, payment-fee schedule, or support workload has been verified. Do not treat this illustration as a current provider quote.

Monthly contribution per account:

`net hosting revenue − server − storage/backups − payment fees − support time × hourly cost − other variable costs`

Keep inference separate. A prepaid balance is not free margin: account for the cost of usage, payment charges, unused-credit obligations, and any support associated with it.

## Illustrative scenarios

Assume €25 revenue before applicable taxes, €1 backup/storage, €1 effective transaction cost, and support valued at €30/hour. These are example inputs only. Taxes collected from customers are not revenue.

| Server/month | 5 support minutes | 15 support minutes | 30 support minutes |
| ------------ | ----------------: | -----------------: | -----------------: |
| €4           |            €16.50 |             €11.50 |              €4.00 |
| €8           |            €12.50 |              €7.50 |              €0.00 |
| €12          |             €8.50 |              €3.50 |             −€4.00 |

The table is contribution before fixed overhead, refunds, abuse, development, and profit taxes. With the illustrative €8 server, only €15 remains before support. Thirty minutes of €30/hour support consumes it all.

If €25 is instead a tax-inclusive customer total, first remove the applicable tax from that €25, then recalculate. Do not assume the same tax rate for every customer or enable a Stripe tax setting without verifying the business setup.

## Evidence needed before opening the full cohort

1. Run the actual agent on the intended Finnish plan; record memory/CPU use, disk growth, and idle/active behavior for a normal week.
2. Quote server, storage, snapshots/backups, and any monitoring costs from the actual account and region. Check https://upcloud.com/pricing/ as a starting point, not a substitute for the chosen configuration.
3. Read the payment fees applicable to the user's Stripe account and intended payment methods.
4. Time setup and support for the first accounts. Count founder labor even when it is not cash-paid.
5. Separate one-off onboarding from recurring support. Price the first year using both.
6. Open the 50-person offer only after contribution is positive under measured ordinary usage. If it is not, adjust the offer before taking new payments; honor any existing price commitment.
