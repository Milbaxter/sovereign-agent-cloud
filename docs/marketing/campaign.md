## Reply bank

**Why pay when other agents are free?**
You’re paying for managed hosting and a service built around choosing your model provider and keeping access to your context. The useful task is the starting point; the control is the reason to choose this version.

**Is everything processed in Finland?**
The agent’s hosting location and the model’s processing location are separate. The final provider information will show where each part runs. BYOK follows your selected provider’s policies.

**Can I run it locally?**
That is the direction. The current portability claim is data export. We are not claiming a finished local-install or full migration path yet.

**Is the whole product open source?**
The platform code is public under Apache-2.0 at https://github.com/Milbaxter/sovereign-agent-cloud. Our goal includes local models, local inference, and data under your control. Production deployment and complete migration still need acceptance testing. OpenClaw and individual models retain their own licenses; open weights alone do not establish that the entire stack is open source.

**Are you saying Muse sells my conversations to advertisers?**
No. Meta says Muse conversations and VM data are not shared with its advertising systems. Our argument is about the control and choices we offer, not an allegation about competitors.

**What happens after 12 months?**
The founding base price is protected for your first 12 months. Any later price and notice terms must be disclosed before signup. We are not promising a lifetime price.

## Why this is still a business

We charge for making ownership convenient: setup, hosting, maintenance, and support. Customers should be able to choose our service because it is useful and leave when another approach suits them better.

Avoid “we don’t mind going out of business” in customer-facing copy. The underlying commitment is that customers should be able to continue independently if they choose to leave or the company stops operating. That continuity must eventually be demonstrated by a tested restore path, not just promised.
