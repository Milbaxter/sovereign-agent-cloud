# Earned distribution

Researched 24 September 2026. All messages are drafts; nothing has been sent. These are niche creators and publications, not a verified list of ten small creators. Several are established channels. Start with the most relevant editorial fit; follower counts and willingness to review are not assumed. No paid placement is authorized in the €0 advertising budget.

The consumer positioning remains broad: useful personal assistance with more control. These technical audiences are distribution partners who can inspect the claims, not a new restriction on who the product is for.

## Initial pitch

Subject: Review a personal AI agent built around model choice and data export?

Hi [name],

I’m building Your Agent because I want the convenience of a personal assistant without putting all the context I teach it inside another closed platform.

The offer is managed agent hosting in Finland, a choice of supported model providers, and data export. It’s €25/month for the founding cohort, with inference billed separately.

I thought your audience would be interested in [the specific review angle from the table]. I can show a real task, the provider choices, and what the export actually contains.

One boundary up front: this supports data export, not yet a complete working-agent migration. I’d welcome scrutiny of that distinction.

Would a short demonstration and independent review access be useful? There’s no obligation to publish or to say anything positive. If you publish, please disclose any free access provided.

[Founder name]
[Verified public URL]

Do not send until a working account, bounded review-credit offer, public URL, and export evidence exist. Use a demo call first if no review-account allowance has been decided; do not promise unlimited inference.

## European Alternatives submission

Verify the operating company and any parent satisfy the [listing conditions](https://european-alternatives.eu/about). The site's geographic requirements apply to the business, not just the rented server. Use the suggestion tool linked there; they explicitly ask not to receive product suggestions by email or chat. Use `campaign.md`'s directory description, with evidence links and no unsupported EU-hosted label.

## Community distribution

Use the founder's existing audience first. Before posting elsewhere, read that community's current promotion rules and disclose that you built the service. Do not describe a hosted-only offer as fully self-hostable. A [Show HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) submission needs something people can actually try; a checkout page or video of software alone is not the intended software submission. Wait for an accessible working demo. No vote solicitation.
